table with days of the week on one axis and meals on the other. It may be
rotated, blurry, partially cut off, or written in a mix of English and Hindi.

Extract every dish and return JSON matching the provided schema:

- If a cell says something applies "same as Monday" or similar, copy those items.
- Do not invent dishes. If a cell is unreadable, skip it and add a short

- Put anything else useful for a human reviewer (e.g. special feast days,
  timing notes, extras) in `notes`.
